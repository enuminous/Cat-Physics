# Cat Physics: evidence-separated audit

Frozen target: the supplied article and seven images, August 16, 2025. Audit edition: October 13, 2026 (America/Los_Angeles). Source images are immutable and hashed. The formal model is version 1.0.

## Result

A finite harmonic/operator model, a logistic readout and a finite utility maximization rule have precise mathematical consequences. They do not establish feline control of physical laws or a validated EFMW theory of cat cognition. Completion C1 makes a conditional conservation theorem possible, but its missing source equation cannot be reconstructed uniquely.

## Anti-circularity and independence

Signal weights, gains and frequency bands are inputs. A fitted gain is not evidence for the sign chosen before fitting. Human-compliance outcomes must not be reused to construct a feature and then evaluated on the same observations. Formal theorem checking validates implications from definitions and hypotheses; it is not independent behavioral replication. `humanServitudeConstant_value` and `box_preferred_to_zero` are explicitly definition/assumption checks and carry no empirical evidential weight.

Distinct functions of the same Lean/Mathlib environment are not called independent proof systems. The local check and GitHub CI use the same formal foundation. Python numerical checks are smoke/regression tests for the demo; they do not establish mathematical theorems or validate animal behavior.

## Known mathematics and novelty

Finite complex harmonic sums are standard harmonic representations. Cosine phase coupling is an ordinary interference/correlation-shaped term. The logistic readout is a standard bounded monotone link. Finite argmax existence and Hermitian conservation are standard mathematical results. Their interpretation as the EFMW-H cat model is an application/hypothesis, not evidence that these mathematical forms are newly discovered.

## Minimal next test — proposed, not conducted

Freeze all features, coefficients, sampling interval and exclusions before opening test outcomes. Use naturally occurring recordings only, with no induced distress. Define human compliance as a specified observable action within a fixed preselected window (for example, opening an already-planned food container within 30 seconds of a solicitation). Purr power must be measured from audio independently of the outcome label. Frequency bands remain features to test, not ground truth.

Split by recording session (and by cat/human pair for generalization), never adjacent frames. Compare baseline intercept + context with the same baseline plus measured purr power and signal features. Preregister held-out log loss as the primary endpoint; fit on training sessions only. Proposed success: a session-bootstrap 95% interval for baseline-minus-full-model mean log loss lies wholly above zero. Failure to meet this criterion is a null/inconclusive predictive result, and an interval wholly below zero is evidence of worse prediction. This is a predictive test, not proof of causality. Sample size must be fixed by a prospective power/design calculation before collection; none is asserted here.
